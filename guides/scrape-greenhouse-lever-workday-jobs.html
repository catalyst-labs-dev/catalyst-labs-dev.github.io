How to Scrape Greenhouse, Lever and Workday Jobs as JSON

Most companies post jobs on an applicant tracking system (ATS) such as Greenhouse, Lever, Workday or Ashby. Each one has a different page layout and API. Our Career Site Jobs Scraper reads 17 ATS platforms and returns one schema: title, location, remote flag, salary, seniority and apply URL.

Input

Give it company names (it finds the career site) or careers/job-board URLs, such as https://boards.greenhouse.io/<company>, https://jobs.lever.co/<company> or a myworkdayjobs.com link.

Over MCP for AI agents

Useful features

  • Keyword, location, remote-only, posted-since and salary filters
  • Monitoring mode that reports new and closed jobs since the last run
  • Errors, not-found companies and duplicates are not charged

Tested numbers

In our Oct 2026 QA, 52 of 52 per-platform cloud cases passed across 17 platforms. NVIDIA, Bosch and JPMorgan Chase boards together (15,057 jobs) came back in 45 seconds with 0 duplicates, and 745 sampled records had 0 schema violations.

Limits

Name auto-detection can pick a different company that shares the same name (2 of 20 in testing). Pass the careers URL for those.
